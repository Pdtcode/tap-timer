Privacy policy

This policy covers the Tap Timer website, {{.Site.BaseURL}}, only. The Tap Timer app for iPhone and iPad is a separate product with its own app privacy policy.

In short

  • There are no accounts and no sign-up. You can play without giving us anything.
  • Your settings and Party names stay in your browser.
  • In online rooms, the name you type and your times are shared with the other players in that room, and kept only while the room is open.
  • The site shows Google ads, which use cookies. Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first.
  • We don't sell your information.

Stored in your browser

The site saves a few things on your device so it remembers you. They never leave your device unless noted, and clearing this site's data in your browser removes them.

  • Until you clear them (local storage): your chosen goal time, Party mode player names and goal, and the name you last used in online rooms.
  • Until you close the tab (session storage): a random ID that lets you get your seat back if you reload during an online room, the code of the room you're in, and whether you've already seen the app suggestion.
  • Only while the page is open: your Best, Average and Rounds stats. They reset when you refresh or leave.

Online rooms

When you create or join a room at /online, your browser sends our server:

  • the name you type, which every player in the room sees (so don't use your full name);
  • your time for each round, which other players see only when the round is revealed;
  • the random seat ID described above.

This is kept in the server's memory only, never written to disk or logs, and is gone when the room closes: after 30 minutes without activity, or when the server restarts. Room links and QR codes contain only the room code. There's no chat.

Server logs and security

Like most websites, our server records each request: your IP address, the page requested, the time, and the response. When you follow a link to the App Store we also record your browser type and the page you came from, so we can count which links people use. We use logs to keep the site running and to stop abuse.

To prevent abuse of online rooms, the server briefly keeps a count of requests per IP address in memory, for about a minute.

The site is hosted by Fly.io on servers in the United States, which process these logs on our behalf and keep them for a limited time.

Advertising

This website shows ads through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising cookies let Google and its partners serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown. You can consent, decline, or manage the options; if you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads.

Anywhere, you can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Sharing and links to other sites

Result cards are drawn in your browser and aren't uploaded to us. If you use a share button (X, Facebook, WhatsApp, or your device's share sheet), you're sending the result through that service, and its privacy policy applies. The same goes for the App Store once you follow a link there.

Children

This site is for a general audience and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The only thing anyone types is a name for a game, which we don't store.

Your choices and rights

  • Clear this site's data in your browser to remove everything stored on your device.
  • Change your ad choices with the consent message (EEA, UK, Switzerland) or Google Ads Settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete personal information about you. Because there are no accounts, we usually can't link logs to you, but we'll help where we can: contact us below.
