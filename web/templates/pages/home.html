FAQ

Is Tap Timer free?

Yes. The web version is free and supported by ads. The iPhone app is a free download with an optional one-time Pro upgrade. It has no subscriptions and no ads.

How accurate is the timer?

We time your taps with your browser's high-resolution clock and measure from the moment your finger touches the screen. The result is shown to the hundredth of a second.

What's the difference between the website and the app?

On the web you can play Solo and Party (Goal Challenge). The app adds Tournament brackets, Teams mode, more themes, landscape play, sound, and Apple Watch and Apple TV support.

Can I play with a group?

Yes. Party mode lets up to 8 players pass one phone around. Everyone's time stays hidden until the final reveal.
